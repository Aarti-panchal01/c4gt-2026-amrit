## Learnings
- UAT auth rides on the `Jwttoken` **cookie** from `userAuthenticate` (plus the Authorization header) — a header-only call 401s; must carry the cookie.
- UAT enforces **one active session per user** → re-login raises the 5002 dialog; automated flows must click "Yes, logout" to kick.
- Follow-on commits (not amends) on a stacked branch keep the other PRs' diffs clean; amending the base forces a full re-rebase of the stack.
- Playwright can record real `.webm` per-context (`recordVideo`) + capture network — good for honest demo proof (no mocking).
